topic: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists and Weight Management
source_note: paraphrased summary; verify against current ADA/ICMR guidance
---
Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists potentiate glucose-dependent insulin secretion, suppress inappropriate postprandial glucagon release, and decelerate gastric emptying. In extensive clinical trials, this class yields potent average HbA1c reductions between 1.0% and 1.8%, while concurrently promoting meaningful body weight reductions ranging from 3 to 7 kilograms. They are strongly endorsed for overweight or obese adults with type 2 diabetes or documented cardiovascular risk. Primary side effects are gastrointestinal, predominantly transient nausea, vomiting, or altered bowel habits, which typically improve with gradual upward titration. Contraindications include personal or familial history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome type 2. Patients with existing retinopathy require careful ophthalmic monitoring during rapid glycemic improvement.
