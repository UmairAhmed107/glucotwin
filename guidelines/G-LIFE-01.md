topic: Lifestyle Intervention and Post-Meal Physical Activity
source_note: paraphrased summary; verify against current ADA/ICMR guidance
---
Comprehensive lifestyle management forms the bedrock of type 2 diabetes self-care. Routine moderate-intensity aerobic exercise enhances non-insulin-mediated glucose disposal into active skeletal muscle beds via GLUT-4 translocation. Importantly, undertaking light to moderate physical activity—such as a brisk 10 to 20 minute walk immediately following large or carbohydrate-dense meals—dramatically flattens early postprandial glucose excursions, attenuating acute peak rises by 15 to 30 mg/dL without imposing hypoglycemia risks. Structuring at least 150 minutes of weekly aerobic exercise interspersed across multiple days, combined with resistance training twice weekly, generates sustained improvements in systemic insulin sensitivity and reduces cardiovascular risk markers. Behavioral interventions should be tailored to individual mobility constraints and daily routines to maximize long-term adherence and metabolic vitality.
