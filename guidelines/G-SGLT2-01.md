topic: SGLT2 Inhibitors with Cardiorenal Considerations
source_note: paraphrased summary; verify against current ADA/ICMR guidance
---
Sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 (SGLT2) inhibitors facilitate urinary glucose excretion by blocking renal tubular reabsorption. Beyond an expected modest HbA1c reduction of approximately 0.5% to 0.9%, this drug class provides substantial organoprotective benefits for patients living with established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, heart failure, or persistent diabetic kidney disease with albuminuria. Renal thresholds dictate their glycemic potency, which wanes at lower filtration capacities; nonetheless, nephroprotective benefits endure across moderate renal impairment. Necessary clinical monitoring includes baseline and ongoing assessment of estimated glomerular filtration rate, blood pressure, and volume status due to osmotic diuresis. Patients should be counseled on proper perineal hygiene to mitigate fungal mycotic infections and alerted to seek immediate clinical evaluation if atypical symptoms suggestive of euglycemic ketoacidosis appear.
