topic: DPP-4 Inhibitors Tolerability and Glycemic Control
source_note: paraphrased summary; verify against current ADA/ICMR guidance
---
Dipeptidyl peptidase-4 (DPP-4) inhibitors extend the biological half-life of endogenous incretin hormones, facilitating meal-related insulin release while tempering postprandial glucagon secretion. Population-based literature highlights an expected intermediate HbA1c lowering effect between 0.5% and 0.8%. A hallmark of this therapeutic class is exceptional tolerability, weight neutrality, and a minimal inherent propensity for hypoglycemia when prescribed without secretagogues or exogenous insulin. They represent an attractive secondary option for vulnerable individuals or elderly adults where hypoglycemia avoidance is paramount. Most agents in this category necessitate dosage adjustment for renal clearance, with the exception of linagliptin which undergoes non-renal elimination. Rare occurrences of severe joint pain or pancreatitis mandate prompt clinical reassessment and drug cessation if symptoms arise.
